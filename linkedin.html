About
Industrial electrician based in Green Bay, WI, open to relocate in the US. More than five years on electrical install and service, including PLC installation and troubleshooting support at Grede Foundries in Reedsburg. Before that, new construction and service calls with AC Electric, and an apprenticeship at Greenfield Electric. Earlier, CNC quality inspection at Comp X.

Building the controls and software side in public: freeCodeCamp coursework, an embedded companion called Loki, and browser labs for a three-wire motor starter, a shift fault log, a copper voltage-drop estimate, and an orbit sketch.
