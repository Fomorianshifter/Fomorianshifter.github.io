About

Industrial electrician based in Green Bay, WI, open to relocate in the US.

I spent more than five years on electrical install and service work, including PLC installation and troubleshooting support at Grede Foundries in Reedsburg. Before that I worked new construction and service calls with AC Electric and apprenticed at Greenfield Electric. Earlier, I inspected CNC work at Comp X.

I am building the controls and software side in public: freeCodeCamp coursework, an embedded companion project called Loki (C, hardware drivers, Flipper Zero UART), and small browser labs for a three-wire motor starter, a fault log, a copper voltage-drop estimate, and an orbit sketch.
