Single-phase voltage drop

Approximate copper drop: VD = 2 × K × I × D / cmil, with K = 12.9. One-way distance. Not a substitute for NEC Chapter 9 tables or an engineer’s calc.

Default selection is 10 AWG (10,380 cmil). 14 AWG shares that cmil value in this list by mistake — fixed in script by option index.
