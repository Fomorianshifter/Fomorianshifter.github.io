Loki

Embedded dragon companion for Linux single-board computers. The public repo covers a TFT display path, hardware drivers, a local configuration UI, and Flipper Zero UART integration. Topics: embedded-systems, flipper-zero, orange-pi, raspberry-pi, tft-display.

Why it is on the portfolio
