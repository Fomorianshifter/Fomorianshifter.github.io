Loki

Public repo: embedded HAL, TFT and bus drivers, a local config UI, and a dragon life-cycle in C. This page only simulates the stage and mood rules described in that README. It does not flash a board.

  • Stages in the repo notes: egg, hatchling at 50 growth, young at 200, adult at 500.
  • Hardware side of the repo covers GPIO, SPI, I2C, UART, PWM, and drivers for TFT, SD, flash, EEPROM, and Flipper UART.
  • README still has merge-conflict markers. Treat the life-cycle section and the hardware file list as the durable parts.
